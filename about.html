About Home Fitness Finds

Home Fitness Finds makes home workout equipment decisions simpler. We publish practical guides on building small workout spaces, choosing resistance equipment, reading specifications, setting a budget and caring for fitness accessories.

Our aim is useful guidance, not exaggerated promises. We clearly distinguish general research from personal product testing and encourage readers to review safety instructions and choose equipment appropriate for their abilities.
