Start with the essentials

Choose your first resistance bands

Check resistance levels, handle comfort, anchoring instructions, and whether the bands suit your workout before buying.

Build a budget home gym

Start with your training goal, usable floor space, and a spending limit. A mat, bands, and adjustable weights may be enough.

Small-space workout ideas

Measure room dimensions and clearance. Prioritize compact equipment that is easy to store safely.

Strength training basics

Pick movements you can perform comfortably, use manageable resistance, and progress gradually. Seek individualized advice when needed.

Equipment safety checklist

Consider weight limits, floor protection, maintenance, warranty terms, and manufacturer setup instructions.

Recovery and accessories

Choose accessories based on what you actually use—such as mats, towels, and storage—rather than buying every trending gadget.
